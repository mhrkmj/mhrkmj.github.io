Power Train Engineer - Georgia Tech Solar Racing

August 2025 – Present

As a Powertrain Engineer on Georgia Tech Solar Racing, a student-led team that designs, builds, and races solar-powered vehicles in international collegiate competitions, I design and iterate on PCBs using KiCad. My work includes the Breakout Board and the Motor-Sense (MIBIB) board, where I produced schematics, layouts, and bills of materials. On the MIBIB redesign specifically, I reduced the board area by approximately 7% while upgrading all components to in-stock parts and modernizing the layout from Eagle to KiCad.

Beyond PCB work, I validate critical vehicle signals including CAN bus, Hall-effect sensors, and temperature sensors, and developed a structured electrical testing plan covering continuity, connector integrity, and isolation checks to ensure reliable motor operation. I also developed embedded C firmware using circular buffers and moving-average filtering to smooth accelerator pedal input, tuning window size and filter weights to balance responsiveness and stability, validated through on-vehicle flashing and live pedal testing.

Subteam Research Lead - AquaBots, Georgia Tech Vertically Integrated Projects

January 2025 – Present

As Subteam Research Lead on the Prince William Sound Salmon Computer Vision team within Georgia Tech's AquaBots Vertically Integrated Project, I lead research into deep learning models for tracking marine life through image processing. I upgraded the fish-scale image classifier from AlexNet to EfficientNetV2-M in PyTorch, improving test accuracy from 47% to 92.28% through selective layer fine-tuning and optimized training configurations, with the model deployed on Georgia Tech's PACE ICE cluster.

I also reduced model size by approximately 18%, going from 66M to 54M parameters, by migrating from EfficientNet-B7 to EfficientNetV2-M, balancing computational efficiency with classification performance. On the data side, I built end-to-end preprocessing and training pipelines, converting ZED stereo footage into depth-augmented datasets and evaluating performance using standard classification metrics to guide iterative improvements. I am also investigating soft labeling and fuzzy logic approaches to better model the inherent uncertainty in fish-scale age labeling.

F1 Fantasy Analytics System
